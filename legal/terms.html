What Ataste does

Ataste helps a group of friends put together one order from Atas Artisan: members add items, the app splits the bill and writes the WhatsApp message, and the host sends that message to Atas Artisan from their own WhatsApp.

Not affiliated with Atas Artisan

Ataste is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed or operated by Atas Artisan. Orders are placed directly between the host and Atas Artisan; Atas Artisan's prices, stock, delivery areas, cut-off times and policies apply. The menu in Ataste is copied from Atas Artisan's public website and may be out of date; Atas Artisan's confirmation is what counts.

Payments

Ataste does not take, hold or transfer money. Members pay the host directly, by bank transfer or e-wallet, outside the app. The host pays Atas Artisan. Your account

You sign in with Apple. You can also verify your WhatsApp number by sending a code from your own WhatsApp: it earns the Verified badge, and you need it to host a group order. The badge only means the number on the account is yours; it says nothing about bank accounts or payments. Keep your Apple ID and your phone secure. You are responsible for what happens in your account.
