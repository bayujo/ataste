Ataste Privacy Policy

Ataste is an iPhone app that lets friends place a group order from the Atas Artisan bakery in Bali: it collects everyone's items, splits the bill, and writes the WhatsApp order the host sends to Atas. This policy explains what Ataste stores, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Ataste is operated by Johar Kurniawan ("we"). Ataste is an independent app and is not affiliated with Atas Artisan.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics SDKs.
  • We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for anything other than running Ataste.
  • Ataste does not process payments. You transfer money to the host yourself, outside the app.

Who can see your data

  • People in the same group see your name, whether your number is verified, your phone number if you verified one (so the host can message you on WhatsApp), your items and your bill.
  • The host of a group sees the transfer receipts of that group's members. Other members don't.
  • Atas Artisan receives the order message the host chooses to send from their own WhatsApp. It contains the host's name and phone number, the delivery address, and each member's name and items.

Service providers

  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to your Apple ID.
  • Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage; servers in Tokyo, Japan).
  • A server we rent in Singapore (OVH) that receives the WhatsApp verification messages sent to our number and refreshes the Atas Artisan menu. Verification codes and the phone number they came from are deleted within 24 hours.
  • Apple Push Notification service, to deliver notifications.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you send the verification message, and when a host sends an order. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to those messages.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you delete your account. Verification codes are deleted within 24 hours. Transfer receipts and delivery photos are deleted 7 days after upload. Order history stays so your group can see past orders and bills.

Deleting your account

In Ataste, go to Profile, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, your phone number, your items and bills, your payment methods and addresses, and the receipts and delivery photos you uploaded, and disconnects your Apple ID from Ataste. Groups you host are deleted for all their members. You can also ask us to delete your account by writing to the address below.

Children

Ataste is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
